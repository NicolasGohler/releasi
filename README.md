# Releasi

LinkedIn outreach platform for Web3 GTM teams.

Runs multi-account connection and follow-up sequences at scale, tracks every lead touchpoint, and surfaces campaign performance through a live dashboard. Used at [MPM Labs](https://mpmlabs.xyz) across client retainers that generated seven figures in pipeline.

## What it does

- Runs outbound sequences across multiple LinkedIn accounts with proxy rotation
- Enriches leads with contact details and Telegram handles for warm follow-up
- Tracks lead status, notes, and attribution from first touch through reply
- Dashboard gives full visibility into campaign activity, lead lists, and weekly KPIs
- Includes a fundraising sub-agent for automated investor prospecting

## Stack

Python, FastAPI, Playwright, Next.js, Docker
